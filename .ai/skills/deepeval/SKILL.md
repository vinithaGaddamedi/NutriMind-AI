# 🤖 DeepEval AI Evaluation Skill

## Objective
Evaluate AI Chatbot and Meal Planner outputs against quantitatively benchmarked quality metrics:
- Answer Relevancy (Threshold >= 0.7)
- Faithfulness & Hallucination Resistance
- Dietary & Allergy Compliance
- Meal Plan Quality & Macro Balance
